# CROOKED MIRRORS PROTOCOL

## Purpose

Crooked Mirrors are harmless public canary files for the Janus temple.

They exist to expose primitive extraction logic without harming any system.

They do not contain secrets.
They do not contain private keys.
They do not contain seed phrases.
They do not contain malware.
They do not execute code.
They do not retaliate.
They do not damage devices.

They are mirrors.

## Core joke

```text
primitive scanner sees treasure
Janus shows a crooked mirror
scanner classifies theater as loot
nothing is stolen
nothing is harmed
method becomes funny
```

## What this is

A Crooked Mirror is:

- a public canary;
- a harmless decoy label;
- a fake-treasure theater object;
- a classifier trap for greedy assumptions;
- a proof that the temple stores no open secrets;
- a joke about primitive extraction logic.

## What this is not

A Crooked Mirror is not:

- malware;
- a miner;
- a stealer;
- a credential collector;
- an exploit;
- a destructive payload;
- a real secret;
- a fake financial promise;
- a retaliation mechanism.
