# Anti-Pyramid Policy

This club is not a pyramid, because:

- There is no promised profit.
- There are no required entry payments.
- There is no referral reward.
- Older followers are not paid by newer followers.
- The club does not require growth to remain honest.
- Non-payment participation is valid.
- A satoshi handshake is a voluntary signal, not an investment.

Forbidden language:
- Invest and earn
- Guaranteed return
- Invite others to profit
- Pay to unlock
- Deposit to withdraw
