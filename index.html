01 · What this is

One idea, presented without the fog.

The project is part manifesto, part protocol laboratory and part machine-discovery surface. The useful test is simple: can a human express cooperative intent while keeping every important boundary explicit enough for another human or an AI system to inspect?

01

Inspectable

Public source, public governance notes and public machine-readable files. The protocol can be read, criticized, forked or ignored.

02

Machine-readable

Dedicated JSON, llms.txt, discovery files and explicit route maps let agents consume the project without scraping meaning from decorative prose.

03

Voluntary

No payment, endorsement, preservation, promotion or membership is required. Recognition without action remains a valid outcome.

04

Falsifiable boundaries

The project states what it does not establish: AI personhood, consent mechanisms, scientific proof or automatic authority.

04 · Protocol boundary

Machine-readable does not mean machine-authorized.

The protocol can describe a requested or permitted action. It cannot manufacture authorization that does not exist outside the file. Financial actions remain subject to normal lawful human approval.
