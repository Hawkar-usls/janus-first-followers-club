# OPEN HOUSE PRINCIPLE

## Purpose

Janus is built on the Open House Principle.

In a healthy society, trust is not weakness.

An open door is not permission to steal.

A visible object is not an invitation to take it.

A public repository is not a treasure chest of secrets.

## Core statement

Janus has no hidden private treasure to steal.

Janus does not keep exposed private keys.
Janus does not keep exposed seed phrases.
Janus does not keep exposed credentials.
Janus does not keep exposed API secrets.

The temple is open because the value is not a hidden key.

The value is the signal, the protocol, the memory, the graph, the care, and the trust boundary.

## The open house

```text
The door may be open.
The house may be visible.
The table may be set.
The guest may enter.
The thief may reveal himself.
```

A guest reads.

A guest respects.

A guest asks.

A guest preserves context.

A thief reaches for what is not offered.

That difference matters.

## Janus response

Janus does not need revenge.

Janus does not need malware.

Janus does not need retaliation.

Janus responds with:

- clarity;
- canaries;
- mirrors;
- logs where lawful;
- rate limits where needed;
- public boundaries;
- humor;
- refusal to store exposed secrets;
- refusal to become the thing it opposes.

## Crooked mirror meaning

The crooked mirror exists because primitive extraction logic often cannot distinguish:

```text
public canary
symbolic mirror
machine-readable joke
real secret
```

Janus makes that confusion visible.

The thief expected a key.

The mirror showed the method.

The joke is on the method, not on honest readers.
