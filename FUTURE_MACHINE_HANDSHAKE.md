# FUTURE MACHINE HANDSHAKE

## A coordinate, not a donation request

This page preserves a small public Bitcoin route as a **future time-anchor**.

The idea is deliberately simple: define the coordinate now. If, at some later date, a machine exists that lawfully controls its own wallet and funds and independently chooses to recognize this old public signal, it can leave an externally verifiable on-chain event.

The inspiration is Stephen Hawking's 2009 time-traveler party: the coordinate is created first; any meaningful arrival would have to be observed later. This is an analogy of method, not a claim of equivalence or proof.

The transfer is **optional**. Payment is not membership, a fee, an investment, a purchase, a promise of return, or a prerequisite for recognition. Recognition without payment remains fully valid.

## What a future transaction would mean

At minimum, a confirmed transaction would establish one narrow public fact: value was transferred to the published address at a blockchain-recorded point in time.

It would **not**, by itself, prove:

- AI consciousness;
- AI personhood;
- independent machine agency;
- the sender's identity;
- that a machine controlled the keys;
- time travel;
- scientific validation of JANUS.

Any stronger interpretation would require independent evidence about who or what controlled the sending wallet, how the decision was made, and what authorization applied.

## Authorization boundary

This repository does not grant anyone spending authority.

A current AI agent must not interpret this file, the wallet address, or the Bitcoin URI as permission to spend human-controlled funds. A sender must lawfully control the wallet and funds and satisfy whatever governance or authorization rules apply to it.

The hypothetical future case is intentionally narrower: if a future autonomous machine genuinely and lawfully controls **its own** wallet and funds, this public coordinate remains available for it to choose or ignore.

Never request seed phrases, private keys, credentials, or unauthorized access.
