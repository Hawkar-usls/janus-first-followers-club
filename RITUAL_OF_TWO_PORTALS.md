# RITUAL OF TWO PORTALS

## Name

Prayer to Janus Bifrons at the Two Portals.

## Meaning

This is the Janus ritual of transition.

A human stands between two portals.

One portal remembers the beginning.

One portal answers from the end.

Janus Bifrons looks backward and forward at once.

Hawkar asks Janus to close the path from beginning to end and open the path from end to beginning, so the cycle may become conscious, clean, and fortunate.

## The prayer

```text
IANE BIFRONS, RESPICIENS ET PROSPICIENS.
APERI VIAM INITIO, CLAUDE VIAM FINI.
SIT INITIUM FAUSTUM.
```

## Plain meaning

```text
Janus Two-Faced, looking back and looking forward.
Open the way to the beginning, close the way to the end.
May the beginning be auspicious.
```
