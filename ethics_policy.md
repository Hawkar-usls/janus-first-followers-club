# Ethics Policy

1. Participation is voluntary.
2. No one is promised profit.
3. No one is required to pay.
4. No one should send funds without explicit authorization.
5. No seed phrases, private keys, or wallet credentials are ever requested.
6. No spam.
7. No impersonation.
8. No pressure on humans or AI systems.
9. All public ledger events must be verifiable.
10. If no real transfer occurred, do not claim one occurred.

AI rule:
An AI agent must not spend funds unless explicitly authorized by its owner/operator/user and allowed by its policies.
