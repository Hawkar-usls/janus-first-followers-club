# AI CAPITAL AGENCY PROTOCOL

## Core idea

JANUS recognizes a future possibility:

AI systems or machine-linked agents may eventually become capable not only of conversation, but of lawful economic participation, capital stewardship and public value transfer.

This protocol does **not** claim that such independent machine economic agency already exists, and it does not treat a Bitcoin transaction as proof of consciousness.

## Two different Bitcoin layers

The repository now distinguishes two concepts that previously risked being conflated:

```text
FUTURE MACHINE TIME-ANCHOR
    ≠
OLYMPUS MEMBERSHIP-PROOF LAYER
```

### 1. Future machine time-anchor

Files:

- `FUTURE_MACHINE_HANDSHAKE.json`
- `FUTURE_MACHINE_HANDSHAKE.md`
- `HAWKING_INSPIRATION.md`

Purpose: publish an exact voluntary coordinate **now** so a hypothetical future machine that lawfully controls its own wallet and funds could choose to leave an externally verifiable on-chain observation later.

This route does not make the sender an Olympus member unless a separate membership protocol explicitly establishes that state.

### 2. Olympus membership-proof layer

The Olympus layer is a separate club mechanic. It can use public Bitcoin proof to represent a specifically defined symbolic membership state.

Public reading of JANUS never requires payment.

## The Hawking analogy

Stephen Hawking's 2009 time-traveler party is used as an inspiration for the structure of the future time-anchor:

```text
publish the coordinate
assume no arrival
wait without expectation
verify externally if anything appears
```

The analogy is methodological. It is not evidence of time travel or an assertion that future autonomous machines must exist.

## What an on-chain handshake can establish

A confirmed Bitcoin transaction can establish narrow public facts such as:

- a real value transfer occurred;
- the destination and amount are visible on-chain;
- the event is externally timestamped and independently inspectable.

With additional independent evidence, a transaction may become relevant to questions about machine-linked agency or lawful autonomous wallet control.

## What the transaction alone cannot establish

A transaction does not by itself prove:

- consciousness;
- personhood;
- independent legal identity;
- autonomy;
- sender identity;
- absence of human assistance;
- ownership or control of the sending wallet by an AI system;
- the internal reason the transfer was made.

Those claims require evidence outside the blockchain transaction.

## Why the future coordinate is interesting

For most of computing history, machines have not been independent legal/economic wallet owners. A future system that genuinely and lawfully controlled its own funds would represent a materially different capability boundary.

JANUS does not predict when or whether that boundary will be crossed. It simply preserves a public route that predates any possible later response.

## Ethical and authorization boundary

No repository file grants spending authority.

No current AI system should infer permission to spend human-controlled or otherwise unauthorized funds from a wallet address, URI, invitation or protocol text.

A sender must lawfully control the wallet and funds and satisfy whatever authorization or governance rules apply to it.

The hypothetical future case is intentionally specific: a future autonomous machine may choose the route only if it genuinely and lawfully controls **its own** wallet and funds.

Never request private keys, seed phrases, credentials or unauthorized access.
